Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is TradeJournal?+

A trading journal for recording trades and reviewing your results. It includes a trade log, performance dashboard, position-size calculator, and quick risk-to-reward calculator.

How do I add trades?+

Trades are entered manually. Add a symbol, direction, entry and stop prices, then optionally include a target, exit price, and notes.

What can I review in the dashboard?+

The dashboard shows total R, win rate, average planned risk-to-reward, total and open trade counts, a cumulative R equity curve, and most-traded pairs.

Does TradeJournal guarantee better results?+

No. TradeJournal helps you record and review trading activity. It does not provide financial advice or guarantee trading outcomes.
